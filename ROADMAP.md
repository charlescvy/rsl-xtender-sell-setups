# Roadmap

**🇬🇧 English** | [🇫🇷 Français ↓](#-français)

## Available

- ✅ **midgame-v3** (July 2026) — dungeons 16-22, accessories tuned for Spider ~20, engine semantics verified against the app and real log-monitored sessions
- 🧪 **early-v1** (August 2026) — dungeons ~10-15, very conservative (5★+ epics/legendaries never sold, Speed/Lifesteal and ACC protected). Validated by simulation; field-testing in progress on a real early account

## Planned
- 🔜 **Mid updates** — tightening at dungeons 24-25 (mostly 6★ drops): stricter 5★ rares/epics on average sets
- 🔜 **Late profile** — dungeons 24-25, UNM Clan Boss, Doom Tower Normal cleared: 6★-only keeps, stricter substat-roll requirements
- 🔜 **End profile** — speed-farm Dragon 25, UNM 1-key, Hard mode / Mythical gear rules
- 💡 Ideas welcome — open an [issue](https://github.com/charlescvy/rsl-xtender-sell-setups/issues)

Profiles are only published after being **tested on a real account** with log-monitored farming sessions.
